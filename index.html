What happens in a call

You bring one recent situation where you're stuck. Not a neatly defined problem—just something that's harder than you think it should be, and your attempts to fix it haven't helped enough.

We spend the session investigating. I'm not here to deliver a productivity tip or convince you that discipline is the answer. I'm here to understand what's actually happening: what you're trying to do, what gets in the way, what you've already tried. We'll look at it together.

Depending on what comes up, we might do a small piece of useful work right there—something you can take away and use. You'll see how I approach the problem: what I'm paying attention to, how I think about it, what I'd suggest trying.

What to know before we talk

This is a free 30-minute session. I won't promise a breakthrough or claim I can fix everything in that time. What I can do is help you understand what's happening and explore whether my approach might be useful for you.

You don't need to prepare or arrive with a solution. You don't even need to know what the real problem is. That's what we investigate together.

If it feels like a fit, we can talk about next steps. If it doesn't, you'll still have spent some time understanding your situation better.
